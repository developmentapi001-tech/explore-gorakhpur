Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Explore Gorakhpur handles information when visitors use this website.

Last Updated: 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Explore Gorakhpur. This website is created to provide information about tourist places, temples, museums, parks, cultural attractions and other destinations in Gorakhpur.

We respect the privacy of visitors and aim to keep the information collected through this website clear and transparent.

2. Information We Collect

Explore Gorakhpur does not intentionally ask visitors to provide sensitive personal information simply to browse the website.

If visitors contact us through a contact form, email or another communication method provided on the website, information voluntarily submitted by the visitor may be received.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information voluntarily provided by visitors may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to visitor questions.
  • Improving the website and its content.
  • Understanding visitor feedback.
  • Maintaining and improving website services.

4. Cookies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies in the future to improve website functionality, understand usage patterns or support advertising services.

If advertising or analytics services are added, those services may have their own privacy and cookie policies.

5. Third-Party Services

The website may contain links to external websites or services such as Google Maps and other third-party platforms.

Explore Gorakhpur does not control the privacy practices of external websites. Visitors should review the privacy policies of those websites separately.

6. External Links

Some pages may provide links to maps, official websites or other external resources to help visitors plan their journey.

Clicking an external link may take visitors to a website that is operated independently from Explore Gorakhpur.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the website secure. However, no internet-based website can guarantee complete security of information transmitted online.

8. Children's Privacy

Explore Gorakhpur is a general tourism information website. We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the website develops or new features and services are introduced.

Any updated version will be published on this page with the latest update information.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the Explore Gorakhpur website, you can contact us through the Contact page.
