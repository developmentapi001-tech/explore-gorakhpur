ABOUT THE TEMPLE

About Vishnu Temple

Vishnu Temple is one of the famous historical and religious temple of Gorakhpur, Uttar Pardesh. It is situated near Asuran Chowk On Medical College Road. The Temple is Dedicated to Lord Vishnu. .

The main attraction of the temple is an ancident four- armed black stone idol of Lord Vishnu. The idol is associated with the Pala Period and is considered an important historical artifact.The Temple has great religious and cultral importance for the people of Gorakhpur.

The temple also has shrine associated with the four impotant pilgrimage places of india---Jagannath Puri, Badrinath, Rameshwaram and Dwarka. Various religious festivels prayers and ceremonies are organized at the temple.

Today, Vishnu Temple is visited by devotees and tourists who come to experience its spritual atmosphere, history and Cultral Significance. It is an impotant part of the Religious heritage of Gorakhpur.

HISTORY

History of Vishnu Temple Gorakhpur

The Vishnu Temple of Gorakhpur is a historic temple located at Asuran Chowk on Medical Road . The temple is famous for its large four-armed idol of Lord Vishnu, made from Black Kasauti stones. Important History

According to historical accounts the Vishnu idol was discovered around 1914 by a local artisan named Gorakh. He initally thought the stone was simplya stones slab.

After the figure was recognized as an idol of Lord Vishnu, it was taken into official custody and eventfully sent to the Lucknow Museum.There were plans during the british period to sent the idol to Londan local people opposed this and effort were made to bring the idol back to Gorakhpur.

Rani Shayam Sundar Kumari of Majhauli Estate played an important role in effort to recover the idol. Historical accounts say that legal effort eventually helped bring it back .

After its return,a Temple was constructed at Asuran in memory of Raja Kaushal Kishore Parsad Mall, and the idol was ceremonially installed there on 8 m .

The temple therefore represents both religious importance and the historical heritage of the city.
