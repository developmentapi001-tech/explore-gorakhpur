ABOUT THE MALL

A Shopping & Entertainment Destination

Orion Mall is located in Mohaddipur, Gorakhpur and brings shopping, food and entertainment together under one roof.

The official mall website describes Orion Mall as a hub for food, shopping and entertainment, with more than 40 national and international brands.

The mall also features restaurants, a food court, cinema and entertainment facilities for visitors.
