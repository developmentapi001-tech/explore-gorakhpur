About Pt. Deen Dayal Upadhyay Park

Pt. Deen Dayal Upadhyay Park is one of the parks listed by the Gorakhpur district administration among places visited by people in the city.

Gorakhpur has several parks and green spaces that provide recreational areas for local residents and visitors.

The official district tourism page includes Pt. Deen Dayal Upadhyay Park along with other Gorakhpur parks such as Govt. Vidhyavasini Park, Indira Bal Vihar, Premchand Park and Nehru Entertainment Park.
