ABOUT THE TEMPLE

About Tarkulha Devi Temple

Tarkulha Devi Temple is a famous religious and historic temple located near Chauri Chaura in Gorakhpur district, Uttar Pradesh. The temple is dedicated to Goddess Tarkulha Devi and is an important place of worship for people from Gorakhpur and nearby areas.

The temple is closely associated with Babu Bandhu Singh, a freedom fighter who took park in the uprising of 1857. According to local tradition, he worshipped Tarkulha Devi as his Ishta Devi and visited the shrine while living and operating in the surrounding forest.

The temple is especially popular during Chitra Navaratri and Ram Navami, when a large annual fair is organized. Thousands of devotees visit the temple during this period to offer prayers and seek the blessings of the goddess.

HISTORY

History of Tarkulha Devi

Historical Background

The temple is strongly associated with Babu Bandhu Singh, a freedom fighter connected with the 1857 uprising. According to local tradition, Tarkulha Devi was his Ishta Devi (chosen deity), and he worshipped the goddess at this site when the surrounding area was covered by forest.

Name of Temple Tradition

The name Tarkulha is traditionally associated with the tarkul (palmyra/palm) tree, which was prominent in the area. Local accounts describe worship of the goddess beneath a tarkul tree before the present temple developed.

Fair and Religious Important

A large annual fair is held around Chaitra Ram Navami, traditionally continuing for about a month. The temple attracts devotees from Gorakhpur and surrounding areas and has becomes an important religious and culture landmark of the region.
