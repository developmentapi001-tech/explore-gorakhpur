MUSEUMS

Zoological Museum

A science museum at D.D.U. Gorakhpur University showcasing the diversity of the animal kingdom.

QUICK INFORMATION

At a Glance

Place Zoological Museum
Institution D.D.U. Gorakhpur University
Department Department of Zoology
Location Pant Block, Gorakhpur
HISTORY

About Zoological Museum

The Zoological Museum is part of the Department of Zoology at D.D.U. Gorakhpur University.

The Department of Zoology was established in 1958. The museum was established with the inception of the department for student teaching and education.

The museum collection has developed through more than six decades of teaching and research activities by students and faculty members.

The museum presents animal diversity in a taxonomic arrangement, allowing students and visitors to study specimens from different groups of the animal kingdom.

COLLECTION

Zoological Collection

🐾 Animal Diversity The museum contains a large collection of zoological specimens including skeletons, preserved animals, birds, reptiles, amphibians, fishes, insects, mollusks, corals and other rare animals.

Things to See

SPECIAL COLLECTION

Rare Zoological Specimens

The museum contains a wide range of specimens representing biological diversity from simple organisms to complex vertebrates.

According to the university, its collection includes preserved poisonous and non-poisonous snakes, marine and freshwater fishes, mollusks, corals and other rare animals.

🦤 Museum Highlight One of the notable displays is a life-size complete stuffed body of an ostrich, along with a rich collection of insects.
EDUCATION

Learning Through Zoology

The museum was established to support student teaching and education through direct study of zoological specimens.

Its displays help students understand animal classification, anatomy, diversity, evolution and biological structures.

Models and charts are also used to illustrate anatomy and life cycles of different animals.

VISITOR INFORMATION

Opening Information

Opening Time 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM
Open On University Working Days

D.D.U. Gorakhpur University states that the Science Museum remains open from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM on university working days. The Government of Uttar Pradesh directory lists entry as free and Sunday and other holidays as closure days. Verify current arrangements before visiting.

HOW TO REACH

How to Reach Zoological Museum

🚆 By Train

Reach Gorakhpur Railway Station and continue towards D.D.U. Gorakhpur University.

🚕 Local Transport

Auto-rickshaws, taxis and other local transport options can be used to reach the university campus.

🚌 By Road

The museum is located in Pant Block of D.D.U. Gorakhpur University, Civil Lines, Gorakhpur.

Tips for Visitors

  • Visit on a university working day.
