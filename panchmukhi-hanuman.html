ABOUT THE TEMPLE

About Panchmukhi Hanuman Mandir

Panchmukhi Hanuman Mandir is a popular Hindu temple located in Taramandal Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh. The temple is dedicated to Lord Hanuman, who is worshipped as a symbol of strength, courage, devation, and protection.

The main attraction of the temple is the Panchmukhi From of lord Hanuman which means "five-faced Hanuman." Devotees visit the temple to offer prayers and seek blessing for peace, strength, and prosperity.

The temple has a peaceful and spritual atmosphere and is visited by devotees throughout the year. Tuesday and Saturday are considered especially important days for worshipping lord hanuman.

The temple is also a place where people come together during religious festivals such as Hanuman Jayanti. it is an important religious and cultural place for the people of Gorakhpur.
