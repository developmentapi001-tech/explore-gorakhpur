HISTORY

History of ISKCON Temple, Gorakhpur

ISKCON was founded by A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada in New York in July 1966. The movement is based on the teachings of the Bhagavad Gita, Srimad Bhagavatam, and the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition.

ISKCON's connection with Gorakhpur

Gorakhpur has a special connection with Srila Prabhupada. He visited the city several times and had plans to establish a Krishna temple and educational activities here. Historical acounts record his visit to Gorakhpur in 1971, when he discussed establishing a Radha-Krishna temple and Krishna-consciousess courses.

The ISKCON Gorakhpur Sri Sri Radha Madhav Temple was established on January 31, 2018, according to the temple's offical website. It is located at Lohiha Vatika, Nand Nagar Colony, Padari Chowk, Gorakhpur.

ABOUT THE TEMPLE

About ISKCON Gorakhpur

ISKCON Sri Sri Radha Madhav Temple is a popular spiritual and cultural center in Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh. The temple is dedicated to Lord Krishna and Srimati Radharani and is part of the International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON).

The present ISKCON Gorakhpur temple was established in 2018. It provides a peaceful environment where devotees and visitors can participate in devotional activities, prayers, kirtans, and spiritual programs.

The temple regularly organizes religious festivals and activities throughout the year. Visitors can experience devotional music, learn about the techings to the Bhagavad Gita, and participate in various spiritual programs.

SPECIAL ACTIVITY

Sunday Feast

The official ISKCON Gorakhpur website invites visitors to its Sunday Love Feast. The program includes kirtan, spiritual discourse and a vegetarian prasadam feast.

Every Sunday The official site lists the Sunday program from 10:30 AM to 1:00 PM.
VISITOR INFORMATION

Temple Timings

The official ISKCON Gorakhpur website currently lists the following daily schedule.

Darshan Reopens 4:00 PM
Darshan Closes 8:30 PM

The official schedule also lists Mantra Japa at 4:30 AM, Mangala Arati at 6:45 AM, Kirtan at 6:30 PM and Gaur Arati at 7:00 PM. Timings can change for festivals and special programs, so check the official temple website before visiting.

How to Reach ISKCON Gorakhpur

🚗 By Road

Travel towards Padari Chowk and use the temple's Google Maps location for the final route.
