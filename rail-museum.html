HISTORY

History of Rail Museum

The Rail Museum, Gorakhpur is a heritage museum dedicated to preserving the hostory and development of the North Eastern Railway (NER).It is located near the Railway Stadium Colony in Gorakhpur. The museum displays historic railway equipment, locomotives, photographs and other items connected with region's railway heritage.

Establishment

The museum was developed to preserve important railway heritage and introduce visitors to the history of the North Eastern Railway. Its foundation stone was laid on 9 April 2005by the then Railway Minister Lalu Prasad Yadav.The museum uses a historic railway bungalow, believed to have been constructed around 1890-1900.

Historical Railway Collection

One of the important exhibits is the historic Lord Lawrence system locomotive, build in 1874. The museum also preserve old cranes, railway equipment, road rollers and other objects showing the development of railway techology.

The museum also has a photo gallery presenting the history and development of the North Eastern Railways. Gorakhpur becomes the headquaters of the North Eastern Railway in 1952, making the city's railway heritage particularly significant.

HISTORY

About the Rail Museum

The Rail Museum in Gorakhpur is a popular heritage attraction dedicated to the history and development of the Indian railway system, especially the North Eastern Railway. It showcase a collection of historic locomotives, railway equipment, photographs, and other objects related to railway heritage.

One of the museum notable exhibits is the historic Lord Lawrence steam locomotive, built in 1874. Visitor can also see old railway machinery and equipment that provide an insight into the evolution of railway techology.

The museum is housed around a historic railway bungalow and also features a photo gallery displaying the history of the North Eastern Railway. A mini toy train and children's recreational facilities make the museum an interesting destination for families and visitors.

The Rail Museum is an important part of Gorakhpur's railway heritage and offers visitor an opportunity to learn about the city's long connection with the railways.

Opening Information

The official Gorakhpur district website states that the museum is open on all days except Monday. Visitor hours and attraction availability can change, so verify the latest information before travelling.

HOW TO REACH

How to Reach Rail Museum

🚆 By Train

Reach Gorakhpur Railway Station and use local transport to travel towards the Railway Stadium Colony area.

🚕 Local Transport

Auto-rickshaws, taxis and other local transport options can be used from different parts of Gorakhpur.

🚌 By Road

The museum is located near Railway Stadium Colony, on the way towards Mohaddipur.
