HISTORY

History of Gita Press

Gita Press, Gorakhpur is a historic publishing institution in Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh. It was established in 1923 with the aim of publishing Hindu religious and spiritual literature at affordable prices.

How to Gita Press Standed

The idea is associated with Jaydayal Goyandka, who regularly participated in satsangs and studied the Bhagavad Gita. After diffculties with externally printed editions, he decided that a dedicated press was needed to produce accurate editions of religious text. His associated Ghamshyam Das Jalan and Hanuman Prasad Poddar were closely involved in the early work.

Gita Press was established in Gorakhpur on 29 April 1923, initially operating from a small rented bulding in Hindi Bazar. A hand-operated printing press was purchased later that year for 600

Important Publications

Gita Press bacame known for publishing works such as the Bhagavad Gita, Ramcharitmanas, Upanishads, Puranas and other spiritual literature. Its monthly Hindi magazine Kalyan began publication in the 1920s and become an important part of its publishing work.

Present Time Gita Press

Over the decades, Gita Press grew from a small printing operation into a major publishing institution. Its own organization says that by December 2025, it had published more than 101 crore books, in 14 languages.

The Government of India selected Gita Press for the Gandhi Peace Prize for 2021.

ABOUT GITA PRESS

About Gita Press

Gita Press is a historic publishing institution located in Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh. Established in 1923, it was founded with the aim of publishing Hindu religious and spiritual literature and making it available to people at affordable prices.

Gita Press is well known for publishing important Hindu scriptures and religious books, including the Bhagavad Gita, Ramcharitmanas, Puranas, Upanishads, and other spiritual works. It also publishes the popular Hindi monthly magazine "Kalyan".

Over the decades, Gita Press has become an important part of Gorakhpur's cultural and publishing heritage. Its historic campus and publications attract visitors interested in Indian literature, religion, history, and traditional printing.

Present, Gita Press remains one of Gorakhpur's best-known cultural landmarks and is an important place to visit for people exploring the city's heritage.
