HISTORY

About Imambara

Imambara is one of the historic places of interest in Gorakhpur and is associated with the city's religious and cultural heritage.

According to the Gorakhpur district administration, the Imambara was built by Hazrat Saint Roshan Ali Shah in 1717 AD.

The monument is known for its association with the religious traditions of the local Muslim community.

The official district website lists Imambara among the important places attracting tourists and local visitors.

HERITAGE

Historical & Cultural Importance
