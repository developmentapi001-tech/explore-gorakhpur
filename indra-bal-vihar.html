ABOUT

About Indira Bal Vihar

Indira Bal Vihar is a recreational park situated in the heart of Gorakhpur city, in the Golghar area.

The official Gorakhpur district website lists Indira Bal Vihar among the city's parks and describes it as a place providing good amusement to children.

Its central city location makes it a convenient recreational destination for local residents and visitors exploring Gorakhpur.
