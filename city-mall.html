ABOUT THE MALL

Shopping & Entertainment in Central Gorakhpur

City Mall is located on Park Road in the Civil Lines and Golghar area of Gorakhpur.

The mall brings shopping, dining and entertainment options together in a central part of the city.

The mall also houses INOX cinema, giving visitors an option for movie entertainment along with shopping and dining.

HOW TO REACH

How to Reach City Mall

🚆 By Train

Reach Gorakhpur Railway Station and continue towards Civil Lines and Golghar using local transport.

🚕 Local Transport

Auto-rickshaws, e-rickshaws, taxis and other local transport options can be used to reach the mall.

🚌 By Road

City Mall is located on Park Road in the Civil Lines and Golghar area of Gorakhpur.
