Archaeological Museum

Explore ancient sculptures, archaeological objects and artistic heritage at the Government Bauddha Museum, Gorakhpur.

HISTORY

About the Archaeological Museum

The Government Bauddha Museum Gorakhpur preserves antiquities collected from the eastern region of Uttar Pradesh.

The museum was established in 1987 and is located in the Ramgarhtal Project Area of Gorakhpur.

The museum's collections reflect the historical and cultural heritage of a region associated with ancient Buddhist, Jain and other spiritual traditions.

The museum works to collect, preserve, document and exhibit cultural property and make it available for educational activities.

COLLECTION

Museum Collections

🏛️ Ancient Art & Archaeological Heritage The museum displays sculptures, terracotta, metal images, pottery, paintings, bronzes and other objects representing India's cultural heritage.
WHAT TO SEE

Things to See

🗿

Ancient Sculptures

See historic sculptures representing Buddhist, Jain and Indian artistic traditions.

🏺

Terracotta & Pottery

Explore terracotta objects and pottery connected with archaeological sites of the region.

🎨

Paintings & Thankas

The museum galleries include miniature paintings and Buddhist Thanka artworks.

🪙

Coins & Historical Objects

The collection also includes punch-marked, Indo-Greek, Kushana, Gupta and later coins.

GALLERIES

Museum Galleries

📜

Rahul Sankrityayan Gallery

Displays Buddhist and Jain art objects ranging from the Kushana period to the medieval age.

🏺

Archaeological Gallery

Features sculptures, stone-age tools, pottery and terracotta from archaeological sites.

🖼️

Miniature Paintings Gallery

Includes miniature paintings from Rajasthani, Pahari and Bundi styles.

🕉️

Jain Gallery

Displays Jain art objects including Tirthankar figures and architectural remains.

VISITOR INFORMATION

Visiting the Museum

The Government Bauddha Museum is located in the Ramgarhtal Project Area, around 6 km south-east of Gorakhpur Railway and Bus Station.

The museum also organises educational programmes such as lectures, seminars and competitions.

Check the latest official visitor information before travelling because timings and visitor arrangements may change.

HOW TO REACH

How to Reach Archaeological Museum

🚆 By Train

Reach Gorakhpur Railway Station and continue towards the Ramgarhtal Project Area.

🚕 Local Transport

Auto-rickshaws, taxis and other local transport options can be used from different parts of Gorakhpur.

🚌 By Road

The museum can be reached by road from Gorakhpur city through the Ramgarhtal area.
