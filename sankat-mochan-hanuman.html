ABOUT THE TEMPLE

About Shri Sankat Mochan Hanuman Mandir

Shri Sankat Mochan Hanuman Mandir is a local Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Hanuman in the Taramandal area of Gorakhpur.

The temple is located along Taramandal Road, in the Rail Vihar Colony Phase 3 and Indira Nagar area.

Devotees visit the temple for prayer, darshan and Hanuman worship.

SPIRITUAL SIGNIFICANCE

Devotion to Lord Hanuman

🙏

Hanuman Worship

The temple is dedicated to Lord Hanuman and provides a place for devotees to offer prayers and seek blessings.

🚩

Sankat Mochan

The name Sankat Mochan refers to Hanuman as the remover of difficulties in Hindu devotional tradition.

🕉️

Prayer & Darshan

Visitors can come for darshan and personal prayer in a devotional environment.

🌺

Local Temple

The temple serves devotees living around the Taramandal and nearby residential areas.
