ABOUT THE PLACE

About Nauka Vihar 2.0

Nauka Vihar 2.0 is a newer recreational destination associated with Gorakhpur's growing lakefront tourism area.

The destination provides visitors with another place to explore around the recreational zone of Gorakhpur.

It can be visited as part of a wider trip covering the Ramgarh Taal and Nauka Vihar attractions.
