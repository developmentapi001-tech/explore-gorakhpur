ABOUT THE PLACE

About Nauka Vihar

Nauka Vihar is a popular recreational destination located around Ramgarh Taal in Gorakhpur. The lakefront area is known for its peaceful atmosphere, open surroundings and recreational activities.

Visitors can enjoy the scenic surroundings of the lake and spend relaxing time with family and friends. Boating is one of the main attractions associated with Nauka Vihar.

TRAVEL TIPS

Tips for Visitors

  • Check boating availability before visiting.
  • Follow all boating and safety instructions.
  • Keep the lake and surrounding area clean.
  • Avoid littering around the water.
  • Carry drinking water during your visit.
  • Keep children under supervision near the water.
