ABOUT THE ZOO

Shaheed Ashfaqullah Khan Prani Udyan

Shaheed Ashfaqullah Khan Prani Udyan is a zoological park in Gorakhpur dedicated to wildlife, nature and public awareness.

The zoo provides visitors with an opportunity to observe animals and birds while spending time in a green outdoor environment.

It is also included among Uttar Pradesh's zoological parks by the Uttar Pradesh Eco-Tourism Development Board.

HOW TO REACH

How to Reach Gorakhpur Zoo

🚆 By Train

Gorakhpur Railway Station is the city's major railway connection. From the station, visitors can continue by local transport.

🚌 By Road

The zoo can be reached through Gorakhpur's city road network using local transport.

🚕 Local Transport

Auto-rickshaws, e-rickshaws, taxis and other local transport options can be used.

TRAVEL TIPS

Tips for Visitors

  • Check the latest opening information before visiting.
  • Follow all zoo rules and visitor instructions.
  • Do not feed or disturb animals.
  • Keep the surroundings clean and use designated dustbins.
  • Carry drinking water and plan enough time for your visit.
