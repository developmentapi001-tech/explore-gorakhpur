ABOUT

About Munshi Premchand Park

Munshi Premchand Park is situated in Alahdadpur, Gorakhpur, an area associated with the noted Hindi writer Munshi Premchand.

The park is a green recreational space where visitors can enjoy the outdoor environment and spend time with family.

The park is also connected with the literary heritage of Gorakhpur through its association with Munshi Premchand.

LITERARY HERITAGE

Premchand's Connection

Munshi Premchand is one of the well-known writers associated with Hindi and Urdu literature.

The location of this park is associated with Premchand's stay in Gorakhpur, making it a place where recreation and literary heritage come together.
