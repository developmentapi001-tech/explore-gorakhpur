ABOUT THE TEMPLE

About Mahadev Jharkhandi

Mahadev Jharkhandi Templeis an old and important Shiva temple in Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh. It is also known as Jharkhandi Mahadev Temple and is listed among the temples of Gorakhpur by the district administration.

The temple is associated with a local tradition that the area was once covered by dense forest. According to the tradition, a Shivling was discovered there, and worship began at the site. Another local account connects its discovery with a londlord named Gabbu Das, who is said to have had a dream about Lord Shiva and subsequently searched for the Shivling with local people. These are tradition accounts rather than independently established historical facts.

The temples attracts many devotees, particularly during Sawan and Mahashivratri, when special worship and gatherings take place. It is regarded as an important religious and culture landmark of Gorakhpur.

SPIRITUAL SIGNIFICANCE

A Place dedicated to Mahadev

Lord Shiva, also known as Mahadev, is one the principle deities worshipped in Hinduism. Shiva temple are important places of worship for devotees across India.

Mahadev Jharkhandi is part of Gorakhpur's religious landscape and is included and in the district administration's list of temples.
