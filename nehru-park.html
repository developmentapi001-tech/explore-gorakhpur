ABOUT

About Nehru Entertainment Park

Nehru Entertainment Park is situated in Laldiggi, within the city of Gorakhpur. It is listed by the Gorakhpur district administration among the city's places of interest.

The park combines recreational attractions with elements connected to the life and history of Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru.

According to the official district website, the children's library, aquarium and photographs of Nehru's life are among the main attractions.

HISTORY

Historical Connection

Nehru Entertainment Park also has a historical connection with the Indian freedom movement.

🇮🇳 Laldiggi Historical Connection The official Gorakhpur district website states that this is the area where Pandit Jawaharlal Nehru was arrested in 1937.
