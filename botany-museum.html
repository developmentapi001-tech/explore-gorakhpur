ABOUT

About Botany Museum

Botany Museum is associated with the Department of Botany at D.D.U. Gorakhpur University.

The museum provides an educational collection related to plant diversity and botanical studies.

The Government of Uttar Pradesh museum directory lists different plant groups, insectivorous plants, local flora and fossil specimens among its collections.

The Department of Botany at the university also maintains a botanical garden with rare, endangered and medicinal plants.

BOTANY & NATURE

Botanical Studies at the University

The Department of Botany at D.D.U. Gorakhpur University was established with the Faculty of Science in 1958.

The department has research and teaching activities in areas including plant ecology, plant physiology, photosynthesis, plant biotechnology and plant pathology.

The university also maintains a botanical garden with rare, endangered and medicinal plants, along with a greenhouse, glass house and aquatic plant area.

🌱 Botanical Garden The university's botanical garden covers approximately 4 hectares and includes rare and medicinal plants, bamboo collections, a greenhouse, glass house and pond.
VISITOR INFORMATION

Opening Information

Opening Time 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM
Closure University Holidays

The Uttar Pradesh Government museum directory lists the Botany Museum as open from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM, closed on university holidays, with free entry. Visitor arrangements may change, so verify the latest information before travelling.

HOW TO REACH

How to Reach Botany Museum

🚆 By Train

Reach Gorakhpur Railway Station and continue towards D.D.U. Gorakhpur University.

🚕 Local Transport

Auto-rickshaws, taxis and other local transport options can be used to reach the university area.

🚌 By Road

D.D.U. Gorakhpur University is located in Civil Lines, Gorakhpur, and is accessible by road.

TRAVEL TIPS

Tips for Visitors

  • Check university holiday information before planning your visit.
  • Entry is listed as free by the Uttar Pradesh Government museum directory.
  • Do not touch protected botanical specimens or displays.
  • Maintain cleanliness around museum and university areas.
  • Students interested in botany can take time to study the specimens.
