Purvayatana Archaeological Museum

A university archaeological museum preserving coins, sculptures, pottery, tools and other archaeological collections.

HISTORY

About Purvayatana Archaeological Museum

Purvayatana Archaeological Museum is associated with the Department of Ancient History, Archaeology & Culture at D.D.U. Gorakhpur University.

The Department of Ancient History, Archaeology & Culture came into existence with the establishment of the University of Gorakhpur in 1957.

According to the university, the department established its museum from 1981–82 to exhibit antiquities found through excavation and related archaeological work.

The museum also supports the academic study of archaeology, numismatics, art and iconography through its collections.

COLLECTION

Archaeological Collection

🏺 Archaeological Heritage The museum collection includes coins, sculptures, photographs, casts and stampages of coins and sculptures, stone tools, pottery and beads.

ACADEMIC HERITAGE

Museum for Archaeological Studies

The museum is connected with the university's teaching and research activities in archaeology and related subjects.

The university states that its collections help students study archaeology, numismatics, art and iconography.

🎓 Learning & Research The departmental museum provides students and researchers with access to archaeological material and helps connect classroom study with genuine historical objects.
VISITOR INFORMATION

Opening Information

Opening Time 10:00 AM – 4:30 PM
Closure University Holidays

The Uttar Pradesh Government museum directory lists the museum as open from 10:00 AM to 4:30 PM, closed on university holidays, with free entry. Verify the latest information before visiting.

HOW TO REACH

How to Reach Purvayatana Museum

🚆 By Train

Reach Gorakhpur Railway Station and continue towards D.D.U. Gorakhpur University.

🚕 Local Transport

Auto-rickshaws, taxis and other local transport options can be used to reach the university area.

🚌 By Road

The university is located in the Civil Lines area of Gorakhpur and can be reached by road.

TRAVEL TIPS

Tips for Visitors

  • Check university holiday information before planning your visit.
  • Follow museum and university visitor guidelines.
  • Do not touch protected archaeological objects or displays.
  • Maintain silence and cleanliness inside the museum area.
  • Students and history enthusiasts can take time to study the collections.
