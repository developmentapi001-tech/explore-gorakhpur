ABOUT

About Ambedkar Park

Ambedkar Park is a public park located in the Taramandal area of Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh.

The park is located along Taramandal Road in the Rail Vihar Colony Phase 3 area.

It can be included among the outdoor recreational places to explore while visiting the Taramandal side of Gorakhpur.
