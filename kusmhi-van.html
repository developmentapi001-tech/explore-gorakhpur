ABOUT THE PLACE

About Kusumhi Vinod Van

Kusumhi Vinod Van is a recreational and picnic destination situated in the Kusmhi Forest area of Gorakhpur.

According to the District Gorakhpur website, the place is situated on National Highway 28, about 9 kilometres from Gorakhpur Railway Station. It is known as a picnic spot and attracts children because of its small zoo containing a few animals.

The surrounding greenery of Kusmhi Forest makes the area suitable for spending time outdoors and enjoying a peaceful natural environment.

HOW TO REACH

How to Reach Kusumhi Vinod Van

🚆 By Train

Reach Gorakhpur Railway Station first. Kusumhi Vinod Van is approximately 9 km from the railway station according to the District Gorakhpur website.

🚗 By Road

The destination is situated on National Highway 28 in the Kusmhi Forest area.

🛺 Local Transport

Local transport such as auto-rickshaws and taxis can be used to travel towards the Kusmhi Forest area.
