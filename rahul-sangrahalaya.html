ABOUT

About Rahul Sangrahalaya

Rahul Sangrahalaya is a museum associated with the Department of Ancient Indian History at Gorakhpur University.

The museum preserves archaeological materials that help visitors and students understand different aspects of the region's history and material culture.

The museum directory of the Government of Uttar Pradesh lists stone, terracotta, pottery, coins and photographs among its collections.

EDUCATION

Learning Through Archaeology

Museum collections provide an opportunity to study physical objects connected with the past rather than learning only through written records.

The museum is particularly relevant for students and visitors interested in archaeology, ancient Indian history and material culture.

🎓 History & Learning Archaeological objects such as pottery, coins, terracotta and stone materials can help visitors understand aspects of past societies and their culture.
VISITOR INFORMATION

Opening Information

Opening Time 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM
Weekly Closure Sunday

The Uttar Pradesh Government museum directory lists Rahul Sangrahalaya as closed on Sundays and other holidays. Entry is listed as free. Photography is allowed with permission from the Head of the Department. Verify current visitor arrangements before travelling.

HOW TO REACH

How to Reach Rahul Sangrahalaya

🚆 By Train

Reach Gorakhpur Railway Station and continue towards the Gorakhpur University area.

🚕 Local Transport

Auto-rickshaws, taxis and other local transport options can be used to reach the university.

🚌 By Road

The museum is associated with Gorakhpur University in the Civil Lines area of the city.

TRAVEL TIPS

Tips for Visitors

  • Check university holidays before planning your visit.
  • Museum entry is listed as free.
  • Photography should be done only with the required permission.
  • Do not touch archaeological objects or protected displays.
  • Maintain cleanliness and follow university visitor guidelines.
