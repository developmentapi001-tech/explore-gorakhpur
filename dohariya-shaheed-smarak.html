HISTORY

About Dohariya Shaheed Smarak

Doharia, in Sahjanwa Tehsil of Gorakhpur district, is associated with an important event of the Quit India Movement of 1942.

On 23 August 1942, a meeting was held at Doharia to register a protest against the British Government.

According to the Gorakhpur district administration, the British response involved firing, in which nine people were killed and hundreds were injured.

A Shaheed Smarak was established in their memory and continues to preserve the historical memory of the event.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE

Doharia and the Quit India Movement

🇮🇳 Freedom Movement Heritage The Doharia event of 23 August 1942 is documented by the Gorakhpur district administration as an important event connected with the Quit India Movement.
