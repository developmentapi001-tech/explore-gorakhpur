ABOUT THE TEMPLE

About Golghar Kali Mandir

Golghar kali Mandir

Is a famous Hindu temple dedicated to Goddess kali, in Golghar,Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh. The temple is an Important place of faith for local people and devotees from nearby areas.

According to loacl belief, the temple is associated with a scared from of Goddess kali that appeared from the Ground. Devotees visit the temple to pray for peace, protection, and fulfillment of their wishes.

The temple is especially croweded during Navratri, when special prayers and religious activities take place. The temple is considered an important religious and cultral landmark of Gorakhpur.

HISTORY & LOCAL TRADITION

The Story of Maa Kali

Golghar kali Mandir is one of the famous religious places inGorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh. According to local belief, a scared from of Goddess kali Appeared from the Ground at this places. People started worshipping the divine from, the gradually the place become a centre of faith.

Later, a temple was a constructed and a statue of Maa kali was installed. With time, the temple become an important religious place for the people of Gorakhpur. Navratriis celebrated with great devotion, and many devotees visit the temple to offer prayers and seek the blessing of Maa kali.

FESTIVAL

Navratri at Golghar Kali Mandir

Navratri is one of the most important periods at the temple. Reports describe large numbers of devotees visiting during both Chaitra and Sharadiya Navratri.

During Navratri, the surrounding area can become especially busy with devotees, पूजा सामग्री and प्रसाद shops.

Navratri A major period of worship and devotional activity at the temple.
