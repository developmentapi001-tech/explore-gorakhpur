ABOUT THE PLACE

About Nauka Vihar 3.0

Nauka Vihar 3.0 is listed as a recreational destination in the Gorakhpur area and is associated with the city's developing outdoor tourism spaces.

The destination provides another option for visitors looking for outdoor recreation and leisure around Gorakhpur.

It can be included in a wider sightseeing plan along with other natural and recreational destinations in the city.
