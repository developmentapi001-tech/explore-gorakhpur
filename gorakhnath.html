HISTORY

History of Gorakhnath Temple

Gorakhnath Temple is one of the most important religious and historical places in Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh. It is closely assciated with Guru Gorakhnath and the Nath tradition of yoga the temple is also an important part of the calture identity of gorakhpur.

1. Guru Gorakhnath

Guru Gorakhnath is regarded as one of the most important yogis of the Nath tradition .The exact date and places of his birth are not historically established.Traditional associate him with the medieval period and he is generally connected with the Nath yogic tradition.

He is traditionlly associated with Matsyendranath who is considered an important predecessor in the Nath tradition.

2.Origin of the Temple

According to the traditionally history og the temple. Guru Gorakhnath performed medition and spiritual practice at the place where the temple now stands.

The place gradually be became an important center of nath tradition .The city of Gorakhpur is also traditionally believed to have recieved its name from Guru Gorakhnath.

The temple's own tradition associate several places in and around the temple complex with Guru Gorakhnath's spritual activities.

3.Development of the Temple

The temple has undergone changes and reconstruction over a long period of time . Traditional account describe period when the temple and damage and later rebuilt.

Historical records and temple traditional do not always prowide the same details about the early history, so some parts of the temple history should be understood as religious traditions rather than family established historical facts.

The 2011 Census District Handbook describes the temple as an ancident religious site and associate the reconstuction of its present from with the late 19th century around 1896.

4. Main Shrine

The main shrine is dedicated to Guru Gorakhnath.The temple complex contains the scared images of Guru Gorakhnath along with his traditional stro footprints (charan paduka).

The temple has developed into a large religious complex and attract devotes from different part of india.

5. Nath Tradition

Gorakhnath temple is an important center of the Nath Sampradaya(Nath traditions).

The Nath traditions emphasizes:

  • Yoga and Meditation
  • Spritual Discipline
  • Self-Control
  • Guru-Discipline Tradition

Gorakhnath is one of the best-known figures associate with his tradions.

6. Makar Sakaranti and khichdi Mela

One of the most famous traditions of Gorakhnath temple is the Khichdi Mela, held during Makar Sakaranti.

Devotes visit the temple and offer khichdi as a religious offering to Guru Gorakhnath According to the gorakhpur district adminiatration the festival traditionally being around 14 january and continues for approxmeately a month.

Large number of two deveotes praticipates in the festivals every year.

7. Important places in the temple complex

Some important places and features associates with the temple include:

  • Main Gorakhnath Shrine
  • Guru Gorakhnath's Charan Paduka
  • Mansarovar
  • Bheem Kund
  • Religious and Meditations area
  • Community food/ meals facilities
  • Cultural and Religious Program

8. Important Of The Temple

Present Gorakhnath Temple is not only a place of worship but also an importtant religious cultural and social center of Gorkhpur.

People visit the temple for worship meditation,festivels and other religious activaties.The temple's Makar Sankranti Khichdi Mela is particularly famous.

ABOUT THE TEMPLE

About Gorakhnath Temple

Gorakhnath Temple is a famous Hindu temple located in Gorakhpur, Uttar Pardesh, India. It is dedicated to Guru Gorakhnath, one of the most important spritual figures of the Nath tradition.

The temple is believed according to tradition to stands at a places associate with Guru Gorakhnath's meditation and spritual practice. Over time it developed into an important center of yoga, meditatiion, spirituality,and religious activities.

The temple complex is large and contains several important religious sites the main shrine is dedicated to Guru Gorakhnath and devotes visit throughout the year to offer prayer and seek blessings.

One of the most famous events at the Khichdi Mela held during Makar Sakaranti. Thousands of devotes participate in this annual religious festivals and offer khichdi at the temple.

Present Gorakhnath temple is an important religious cultural and historical landmark of Gorakhpur. It attract devotes and visitors from different parts of india and plays an important role in the cultural identityof the city.
