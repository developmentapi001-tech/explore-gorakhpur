ABOUT THE TEMPLE

About Budhiya Mai Mandir

Budhiya Mai Temple is a famous religious shrine located in the Kusmhi forest area of Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh. Surrounded by greenery and a peaceful nature environment, the temple is an important place of worship for local devotees.

According to local traditions, the shrine has a long history and is associated with stories about Budhiya Mai, a revered local goddess. The temple's tradition have been passed down through generations, making it an important part of the religious and culture heritage of the Gorakhpur region.

The temple attracts many devotees throughout the year, especially during Navratri. During festivals, the surroundings become lively with religious activiies and Visitors coming to offer prayers and seek blessings.

HISTORY & TRADITION

The Story of Budhiya Mai

Historical Story

According to local tradition, the area was once a dense forest where the Tharu community worshipped a forest deity at three sacred mounds (pindis). People are said to have occasionally seen an elderly woman dressed n white near these sacred places, who would disappear shortly afterward. This figure bacome associated with Budhiya Mai.

One of the best-known legends tells of a wedding procession crossing a wooden brige over the Turra stream. According to the story, an elderly woman asked the performers in the procession to dance. This story is a local religious legend, not an independently verified historical event.

Another local tradition connects the establishment of the shrine with a man named Jokhu Sokha, who is said to have begun worship at the site after an extraordinary experience involving Budhiya Mai.

FESTIVAL

Navratri at Budhiya Mai

Navratri is an important period for Budhiya Mai Mandir. Reports describe large numbers of devotees visiting the temple during both Chaitra and Sharadiya Navratri.

Navratri A major period of worship and pilgrimage at the temple.
