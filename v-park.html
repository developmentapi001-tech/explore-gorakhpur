ABOUT

About Vindhyavasini Park

Vindhyavasini Park, also referred to as Government V-Park, is one of the green parks listed among the places of interest in Gorakhpur.

The official Gorakhpur district website describes the park as a green space inside the town and a place used by morning walkers. It also highlights varieties of plants, trees and fruits found in the park.

The district information also mentions the development of mother buds of roses for different colours.

HOW TO REACH

How to Reach Vindhyavasini Park

🚆 By Train

Reach Gorakhpur by train and continue towards the park using local transport.

🚕 Local Transport

Auto-rickshaws, taxis and other local transport options can be used to reach the park from different parts of the city.

🚌 By Road

The park is located in the Kawwa Bagh Colony area of Gorakhpur.

TRAVEL TIPS

Tips for Visitors

  • Keep the park clean and use designated waste bins.
  • Respect plants, flowers and other park property.
  • Morning and evening can be suitable times for outdoor walks.
  • Keep children supervised during the visit.
  • Check current local information before travelling.
