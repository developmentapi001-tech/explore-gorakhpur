ABOUT

About Herbal Garden

Herbal Garden is associated with the AYUSH Department of All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Gorakhpur.

The official AIIMS Gorakhpur website mentions the Herbal Garden under the AYUSH Department, which focuses on traditional systems of medicine and holistic healthcare.

The garden provides a green setting connected with the awareness and educational aspects of medicinal and traditional plant-based practices.

Herbal Garden & AYUSH
