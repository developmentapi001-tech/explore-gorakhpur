HISTORY

About Chauri Chaura

Chauri Chaura is historically associated with an important incident of India's freedom movement that took place on 4 February 1922.

The incident occurred during the Non-Cooperation Movement. A group of protesters clashed with police after police opened fire.

The incident became an important event in the history of the freedom movement and had a direct impact on the Non-Cooperation Movement.

The Shaheed Smarak commemorates the historical significance of Chauri Chaura.

VISITOR INFORMATION

Visiting Chauri Chaura

Chauri Chaura is connected with Gorakhpur by rail and road. Check current local visitor information before travelling.

HOW TO REACH

How to Reach Chauri Chaura

✈️ By Air

Gorakhpur is the nearest airport mentioned by the district administration for reaching Chauri Chaura.

🚆 By Train

Chauri Chaura is connected by railway, and trains towards Deoria stop at Chauri Chaura.

🚌 By Road

Buses and taxis are available from Gorakhpur and Deoria to reach Chauri Chaura.
